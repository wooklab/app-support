Privacy Policy

Blanc · Effective November 26, 2026

Summary

The developer does not collect any personal information. The app has no accounts, sign-in, ads, or analytics, and it does not connect to the internet.

Photos

The app reads only the photos you choose in the photo picker and does not access your whole photo library. Borders and shooting information are added entirely on your iPhone, and your photos never leave the device.

When saving results to Photos, the app asks for the Add Photos permission. This permission only allows adding photos; it cannot read existing ones. You can change it at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Photos.

Saved photos keep the original shooting information (EXIF), but location (GPS) data is removed. If you send a photo to another app with the Share button, that app's privacy policy applies from then on.

Information stored on your device

The app stores your last chosen border color, layout, aspect ratio, font, and date setting, as well as the photographer name and credit style entered in Settings, only on your iPhone. The app and its share extension share this information, and it is never sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes this information.

Children's privacy

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the changes and the new effective date will be posted on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, please use GitHub Issues. Issues are public, so do not include personal information.
