Blanc

iPhone app

Blanc adds a white or black border to your photos and writes the shooting information in it. Your photo keeps its resolution and colors, and everything happens on your iPhone.

FAQ

Does it reduce photo quality?
Photos saved from the app keep their original resolution and color space. Only very large panoramas whose result would exceed 100 megapixels are scaled down to that size.

Why are photos saved from the Share button smaller?
Features that run from the Share button in Photos must stay within a memory limit set by iOS, so they save a JPEG whose long edge is 2400 px. To keep the original resolution, save from the Blanc app.

The shooting information does not appear.
Photos without EXIF data have nothing to show. Photos received through messaging apps and screenshots often have it removed. You can enter it yourself with the text button at the top of the editor.

Is the location kept?
No. Location (GPS) data is removed when saving. Other shooting information is kept.

I saved several photos, but none were saved.
If any photo fails to save, Blanc saves none of them, so you never end up with a partial set. Check your storage and the Add Photos permission (Settings > Privacy & Security > Photos > Blanc), then try again.
